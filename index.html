The covered market

Under the canopy, rain or shine

Two sloped double-tee canopies cover the market rows along Detroit Street. Vendors unload at the curb, set up under cover and plug into power fed by the canopy's own solar panels.

    The building

    How the Hex is made

    Two precast bars on 12-foot double-tee grids, a hexagonal skin and two sloped canopies that carry the market and 1,169 solar panels.

    Structure + skin

    Precast double tees in three bay sizes, and a hexagonal skin hung on the slab edges.

    58'Large bay · clear double-tee span over both event halls
    46'Medium bay · lobby, offices, classrooms
    19'-6"Small bay · restrooms, slot, services

    Precast columns stand under every double-tee stem. Two concrete cores hold the stairs and elevators. The skin is built in 6-foot bays, inside to outside:

    1. APrecast double-tee slab edges and the 1'-0" sloped roof plane
    2. BInsulated infill panels where the hexagons meet
    3. CInsulated glass in the hex lites and the clerestory band
    4. DHexagonal aluminium frames, 6'-0" flat to flat
    5. EVertical fins on the bay lines

    Energy

    Every south-tilted roof and canopy carries solar panels.

    1,169PV modules · 620 W each
    724.78 kWDC capacity on roofs + canopies
    928,186 kWhper year · about 186% of the 500 MWh target
